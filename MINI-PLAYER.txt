SONORA MINI PLAYER

Extract this ZIP, fully quit any running Sonora (including its tray icon),
and open Sonora-MiniPlayer.exe. This portable copy uses your existing Sonora
settings and library; it does not replace the installed app.

Open the mini player with Ctrl+Shift+M or the mini-player icon in Now Playing.
Drag the album cover freely. Hover for playback controls. Press Escape or
the return button to return to the full app.

RIGHT-CLICK THE COVER
Position: Top left, Top middle, Top right, Bottom left, Bottom middle,
or Bottom right. Alignment uses the current monitor and leaves a gap from
the screen edges and taskbar. You can drag away from any position.

Cover size: Small, Medium, or Large.
Always on top: Check to keep the cover above other apps; uncheck for normal layering.

Position, size, and always-on-top preference are saved automatically.
Scroll up over the cover to increase volume, or down to decrease it.

SECOND MINI PLAYER
Press Ctrl+Shift+N in Sonora to open a separate compact card, or choose
Open compact player from the artwork-only player's right-click menu.
The original Ctrl+Shift+M artwork player keeps its existing appearance and controls.
Both players can be open at once. They share the same music playback.

The compact card has small artwork, track and album details, a seek bar,
elapsed and remaining time, mute/volume, previous/play/next, and player options.
Its lyrics and queue buttons open the existing Sonora panels.
Play/pause icons fade between states; artwork gently shrinks when paused and returns when playing.
Controls animate on hover and click. These short transitions follow Sonora's reduced-motion setting.
Drag its artwork/title area to move it. Scroll over the card to adjust volume.
Right-click the card or select its three-dot button for position, size, and
always-on-top options. Its position, size, and topmost setting are saved separately.
Escape or Ctrl+Shift+N closes the card and returns to Sonora.

CONTROLLER SHORTCUTS (WINDOWS)
Xbox: hold View (the overlapping-squares button), then tap RB for next or LB for previous.
DualSense: hold Create (left of the touchpad), then tap R1 for next or L1 for previous.
Hold the modifier first, then tap a bumper. Holding a bumper does not repeat.
Works while Sonora is minimized or in the tray. Both mini players keep their existing controls.
Turn this off in Settings > Playback > Controller shortcuts.
Sonora only reads controller input; the game still receives these buttons too.
USB and Bluetooth DualSense reports and Xbox-compatible XInput controllers are supported.
If another tool hides the physical DualSense, its Xbox-emulation output can be used instead.

SHARING
Keep LICENSE, THIRD-PARTY.md, and SOURCE.txt with the app when sharing.
SOURCE.txt identifies the exact matching source code for this custom build.
Your friend uses their own music accounts and files. Online music services
still require an internet connection and their usual account/subscription.

This is a modified Sonora 0.43.0 build. Official Sonora updates do not
automatically contain these custom mini-player changes.
